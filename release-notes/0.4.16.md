## New

- **The plan of your project in the Workbench.** CodeDeck reads ROADMAP.md, TODO.md and IDEAS.md and shows on top what is next: the stage being worked on with its steps, the next step with one click to attach it to your next prompt, the other stages in progress with what is left (or that only the practical test is), and the open points, tasks and ideas. Without a roadmap the next TODO entries or ideas show as cards. The project list shows the plan in one line under each name.
- **Guide files.** Instructions for the AI, README, TODO, IDEAS and ROADMAP stand at the top of the file list with their own symbol. Which names count is set in Settings → Files and folders; a project chooses its own from a file's menu (Guide file).
- **The Workbench in zones.** To do gathers what you asked of the AI and is not done yet (@ai hints, line comments, open points of the TODO file); New shows what came up since you last looked, by AI session, pictures as thumbnails. What sessions put outside the project and the scratch folder are folded at the bottom.
- **The scratch folder tidies itself.** Old files go to the trash after a number of days per kind (drafts 30, other files 14, the rest 7; changeable or off in Settings → Files and folders). What you keep (bookmark), attach, have not seen yet or name in a guide file stays; the bell tells you what went. Backup folders such as a copied repository count and expire as one. A kind in the scratch line shows those files in the file list.
- **Clickable text looks clickable**: links and things that open show a soft surface on hover, their symbol in the accent colour.
