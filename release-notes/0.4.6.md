## Changed

- **An ended conversation is continued on its own card, and only there.** The Play buttons that continued "the last conversation" are gone: they skipped sessions that had ended cleanly and then started a new conversation without saying so. "+" still starts a new one. When a card cannot continue, it says why (tmux missing, no terminal found, or the AI tool cannot do it).
- **The way into a session is a marker at rest.** It shows whether a double click opens the desktop app or a terminal; the buttons are the hover tools. "Answer" stays a button while a session asks.
- **The terminal way brings an open terminal to the front** when one already shows the session's tmux session (X11), instead of opening another. Elsewhere it opens a new one as before.

## Fixed

- **The last row of a session card is no longer cut off in a narrow column**, and the hover tools show whole: labels shrink to icons.
