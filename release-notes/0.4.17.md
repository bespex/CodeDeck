## Changed

- **The Workbench in calm cards.** Plan, To do and New are cards with a quiet head that names what is in them in one sentence. No colour for decoration: the state of a step shows by its shape (filled done, a ring with a centre in progress, an empty ring open), groups have small headings, marks have a frame.

## Fixed

- **New files showed up late or not at all** when the scratch folder held a large backup (a copied repository): its thousands of files filled the list. A backup folder now counts as one entry; it is no longer shown in the Workbench, but tidying up and expiry still handle it.
