## Fixed

- **Project groups stay apart**: with your home folder (or any folder above the others) on the list, every project fell into one group named after it. A project's group is now the deepest folder that holds another of your projects, wherever the rest lie: `~/projects/bespex/…` make "bespex", `~/projects/tsobss` with its projects makes "tsobss", and projects right in `~/projects` make "projects". A project in `~/Dokumente/Setup/…` no longer flattens the others.
- Two group folders of the same name are told apart by their parent folder.
