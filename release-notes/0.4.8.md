## Changed

- **A limit that is nearly used up shows it.** From 90 % the icon, figure and bar turn amber ("almost used up"), however much of the time is left. At 99 % the week used to look as calm as at 10 %.
- **An account's configuration folder has its own line in the settings,** in full, instead of being cut off beside the name. Its tooltip names the file the hooks are put in.
