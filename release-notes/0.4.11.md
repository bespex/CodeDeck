## New

- **Comments in HTML pages.** Opening an HTML file of the project in Hyqe (0.3.0 or newer) now lets you comment on its elements, as in Marq: point at a paragraph or a button and press “+”. `@ai` comments show up as hints, also where Hyqe puts them inside a line (`<li><!-- @ai: … --><p>`) and over several lines; the task for the AI says that such a comment is about the element that follows it.
- **Themes from the be spex kit.** Colour theme and style are the same choice as in every be spex app, on a tab of their own in the settings (“Theme”): twelve themes in three styles, with the axes that change something here (shape, structure, colour of regions, effects). A theme chosen before is taken over.
- **Events as a setting of their own:** quiet, distinct (the new default) or loud.

## Changed

- The project list in the project window is set apart from the sessions: it has the colour of the files column.
